Bacalar has no commercial airport. To get there you have three reasonable starting points — Cancún (CUN), Tulum (TQO, opened late 2023), and Chetumal (CTM). Each one changes the conversation: distance, price, and how late you can land before things get awkward.

Before the details, an honest recommendation: if you're traveling with luggage, with a group, or with no appetite for coordinating public transport after a flight, **book a private transfer before you board the plane**. The price gap with a colectivo, or with the Uber-to-Tulum-then-shuttle combo, doesn't make up for the time and uncertainty — and local drivers will track almost any flight time of day with no surcharge if you book ahead.

## From Cancún (CUN) — the most common route

Cancún has the most international flights and is where most travelers land. The distance to Bacalar is **~330 km** (about 4 to 4.5 hours by road via the 307 + 293).

Transport options:

- **Door-to-door private transfer** — the only option that puts a person waiting for you with a sign at arrivals. For 1–4 passengers it runs USD $300–$380 depending on the vehicle. For larger groups (6–10 pax van) it's USD $450–$550.
- **ADO direct bus** — leaves the airport a few times a day, takes ~5 hours, costs ~$650 MXN. Cheapest but rigid schedule and won't wait for delayed flights.
- **Uber + colectivo combo** — Uber to Tulum (~$1,500 MXN), then colectivo Tulum-to-Bacalar (~$250 MXN). Only worth it if you land early and enjoy the coordination.

[Book your private transfer from Cancún](/transfers) if you want it locked in before you land — local drivers track your flight by the number you give them and adjust pickup time if your flight shifts.

## From Tulum (TQO) — newest option, fastest drive

Tulum's Felipe Carrillo Puerto airport opened late 2023 with direct flights from several Mexican and US cities. The drive to Bacalar is **~210 km** (about 2.5 to 3 hours by the 307).

Most comfortable option if your flights line up:

- **Door-to-door private transfer** — for 1–4 pax USD $200–$260, for groups USD $320–$400. Booking ahead matters more here because fewer operators work this airport regularly.
- **Rental car** — viable if you'll be exploring the region after. Roads are good, driving is straightforward. Add gas, tolls, parking in Bacalar, and the rental itself though, and the math comes out close to the private transfer.

## From Chetumal (CTM) — closest, but limited

Chetumal is just **~40 km from Bacalar** (about 40–50 minutes by road). The catch is the airport handles very few flights: mainly Mexico City and a few connections. If your international itinerary doesn't include CDMX, it's probably not an option.

When it does fit, it's clearly the easiest:

- **Private transfer** — runs USD $90–$140 depending on the vehicle. Door-to-door in about an hour.
- **Local taxi** — available at arrivals, ~$700–900 MXN. No prior booking needed, but some drivers don't go beyond the Chetumal urban area — worth confirming before getting in.

## Once you arrive

Most Bacalar hotels are along Avenida Costera (the road parallel to the lagoon) or on the way to Cocalitos south of town. The town center is small and walkable; for the southern zone (Cocalitos, Los Rápidos) you'll want a local taxi or the [round-trip Bacalar Taxi service](/transfers) if you'll use it more than once.

If you want to start your first day right, the [Bacalar 101 tour](/tour/bacalar-101-private-tour-of-the-lagoon-of-the-7-colors) is the most popular first-day option — early start, hotel pickup, and shows you the full lagoon before the afternoon heat.
